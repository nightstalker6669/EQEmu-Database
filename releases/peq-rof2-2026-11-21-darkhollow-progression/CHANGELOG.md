## Depths of Darkhollow progression

- Finished the Longshadow questline from Freeing an Elder through Confronting a Traitor, including the Orb of Subversion, A Plea for Help, and Trailing Longshadow. Mission items, hand-ins, encounters, and return routes now track the owning task and instance.
- Restored the four required Bellfast missions and four required Widdlethorpe missions. Neran's spell now records each member's crossing when they land on the dry far shore, and the Temple verifies that member's crossing before admitting them.
- Restored the Monocle of Blood path, including Dreadspire access steps, the Library, Eyes, and Memories missions, the slurry combine, and the Coldwind and public Vule steps.
- Restored the five Blood raids and the core Demiplane of Blood progression through Mayong Mistmoore. The update includes encounter phases, corpse loot, key hand-ins, and victory lockouts.
- Completed the Skull of Den Lord Rakban progression. Earn and claim the five fragments and the shroud, then combine the fragments for the Ancient Werewolf Skull and the linked achievement.
